Research & Publications

My research focuses on groundwater exploration, geospatial intelligence, DEM-based terrain analysis, remote sensing, and environmental monitoring. I have published peer‑reviewed papers, contributed to international conferences, and collaborated on multidisciplinary geoscience projects.

Groundwater Potential Mapping Using DEM-Derived Lineaments & Remote Sensing
China University of Mining and Technology (CUMT), Xuzhou, China
MSc Research
  • Developed a groundwater potential model integrating DEM-derived lineaments, lithology, slope, drainage density, and land-use/land-cover.
  • Applied advanced geospatial techniques including shaded relief analysis, automatic lineament extraction, and multi-criteria evaluation.
  • Performed spatial modeling using GIS and remote sensing to identify groundwater recharge zones and structural controls.
  • Produced high-resolution maps and 3D visualizations for hydrogeological interpretation.
  • Published research findings in peer-reviewed journals and presented at international conferences.
Geological Mapping & Structural Analysis of Basement Complex Terrain
Federal University of Technology Akure (FUTA), Nigeria
B.Tech Research
  • Conducted field mapping, structural measurements, petrographic analysis, and geological interpretation of basement complex rocks.
  • Integrated remote sensing data with field observations to improve lithological boundary identification.
  • Produced detailed geological maps, cross-sections, and structural models.
