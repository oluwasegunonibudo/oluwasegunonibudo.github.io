Research & Publications

My research focuses on groundwater exploration, geospatial intelligence, DEM‑based terrain analysis, remote sensing, and environmental monitoring. I have contributed to scientific studies involving lineament extraction, hydrogeological mapping, deep learning applications in geoscience, and multi‑criteria groundwater modeling. My work has been published in peer‑reviewed journals and presented at international conferences, supporting sustainable groundwater development and advancing geospatial analytical methods.
