About Me

I am Oluwasegun Onibudo, a Geospatial Analyst and Applied Geologist with a strong multidisciplinary background in GIS, remote sensing, cartography, and earth science. My academic foundation includes a B.Tech in Applied Geology (FUTA, Nigeria) and an MSc in Cartography & GIS (China University of Mining and Technology, CUMT), where I conducted advanced research in groundwater prediction, DEM‑derived lineament extraction, and geospatial modelling.

With over seven years of hands‑on experience, I have contributed to major geospatial and land‑administration projects across Nigeria and China, including OYOGIS, KADGIS, NASRDA, and Onidex Geo‑Spatial Solutions. My work spans groundwater exploration, environmental monitoring, wetland mapping, spatial modelling, land‑use/land‑cover change detection, and digital land administration. I am proficient in industry‑standard tools such as ArcGIS, QGIS, ERDAS Imagine, ILWIS, ENVI, PCI Geomatica, Rockworks, Google Earth, and Python.

I am also a published researcher with multiple peer‑reviewed journal articles and international conference presentations. My research contributions include groundwater targeting using satellite imagery, automatic lineament extraction, shaded relief analysis, and deep learning applications in geoscience. I have received notable recognitions, including the Best Student Paper Award at the XVIII International Congress for Mine Surveying (Xuzhou, China).

Beyond research and project execution, I am deeply committed to capacity building. I have led geospatial intelligence seminars, GIS training workshops, and professional development programs for students, government agencies, and industry professionals-helping strengthen geospatial literacy and digital land‑administration practices across Nigeria and China.

This portfolio reflects my journey across geology, geospatial science, research, teaching, and professional practice-showcasing the maps, projects, publications, and engagements that define my commitment to using geospatial technology for sustainable development and informed decision‑making.
