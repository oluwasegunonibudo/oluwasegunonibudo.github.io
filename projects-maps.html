Projects

A collection of applied geospatial projects involving GIS analysis, remote sensing, cartography, spatial modelling, environmental monitoring, land administration, and geospatial intelligence.

Groundwater Lineament Mapping

Using GIS and Remote Sensing to map structural lineaments for groundwater resource assessment.

Malaria Incidence Mapping

Spatial analysis of malaria cases across Akure using hospital‑based health data and geospatial modelling.

Accident Mapping & Emergency Response Routing

Identifying optimal routes to nearest hospitals based on road networks, travel time, and accessibility.

Crime Mapping of Abuja Three‑Arm Zone

Integrating field observations, questionnaires, and GIS analysis to map crime patterns for security planning.

Facility Monitoring in Abuja, Nigeria

Monitoring and mapping critical facilities across the Abuja metropolis using GPS field data, satellite imagery, and GIS workflows. The project involved field verification, spatial data collection, and geospatial analysis to support infrastructure management, security planning, and urban development.
